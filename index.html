How the Champions SP to EV conversion works

Stat points to EVs

At level 50 one Champions stat point adds one point to the stat. In the regular games the first point in a stat costs 4 EVs and each one after that costs 8, so 1 SP = 4 EVs, 2 SP = 12 EVs and 32 SP = 252 EVs.

Why 66 stat points can exceed the 510 EV limit

Champions gives every Pokémon 66 stat points. Put into four or fewer stats, they come out as 512 to 516 EVs, which is over the 510 EV cap of Scarlet/Violet and earlier games. The converter warns you and trims the lowest invested stat so the spread stays legal.

EVs to stat points

Going the other way, each stat becomes (EVs + 4) ÷ 8 rounded down, capped at 32 SP per stat and 66 SP total. EVs that don't land on a full stat point are flagged.

Trainer details for PKHeX

Optional OT, TID, SID, language, ball and shiny settings are written as Auto-Legality Mod lines so the exported team can be imported into PKHeX.
